Privacy Policy for Itinera

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Itinera ("the app", "we", "us") is a travel itinerary and ticket organizer published by YnotLabs. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and your choices. We designed Itinera to collect only what it needs to work.

Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in (including via Google Sign-In), we receive your name and email address to create and secure your account.
  • Your trip content — the trips, legs, activities, tickets, documents, checklist items, and any photos you add are stored so they sync to your account.
  • Photos — if you choose to add a photo to a trip, the app accesses your camera or photo library only for that action. Itinera does not scan your library.
  • Account identifier — a unique user ID is assigned to your account so your data can be associated with you.

Nera, the AI trip planner

Itinera includes an optional assistant called Nera that helps you plan trips. Nera is only used when you open it and send a message.

  • What is sent — the messages you type to Nera, the draft itinerary you are reviewing, and today's date. Please don't include sensitive personal information in your messages. Your name, email address, documents, tickets, expenses and existing trips are not sent.
  • Who processes it — your messages are sent through our server to Anthropic, an AI provider, which generates Nera's replies. To answer weather questions, the city and dates are sent to Open-Meteo; to find restaurants and attractions, a search phrase and city are sent to Google Places. None of these requests include your name or email.
  • What we keep — your conversation with Nera is not saved on our servers and is cleared when you leave the chat. We store only counts of how many Nera requests your account has made each day and month (to prevent abuse), how many trips you have created with Nera (to apply the free-trip limit), whether you have unlocked Nera Plus, and a cache of place search results, which contains no information about you. If you approve a draft, it is saved to your account as an ordinary trip like any other.
  • Accuracy — Nera is an AI and can make mistakes. Check opening hours, prices, entry requirements and other details with the official source before you rely on them. Weather far in advance is a guide based on past conditions, not a forecast.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to provide the app's features: authenticating you, storing and syncing your trips and documents, planning trips with Nera when you ask it to, and personalizing settings such as language and currency. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Service providers

Itinera uses the following third-party services to function:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage) — hosts your account and trip data securely. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Frankfurter API — provides currency exchange rates. Requests contain only the currencies being converted, not personal data.
  • Anthropic — the AI provider behind Nera. It receives only the messages described in the Nera section above, and its handling of them is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Google Places API and Open-Meteo — used by Nera to look up places and weather. Requests contain a search phrase or city and dates, not personal data.
  • RevenueCat — processes the one-time Nera Plus purchase together with the App Store or Google Play. It receives your account's random ID and your purchase status, not your name, email, trips or messages.
  • Cloudflare — runs the server that connects the app to Nera. It handles the requests described in the Nera section and does not keep your conversation.
  • Unsplash API — provides destination cover images. Requests contain only a destination search term, not personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can delete your trips at any time within the app. To request deletion of your entire account and associated data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

Data security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Google Firebase's managed infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children's privacy

Itinera is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

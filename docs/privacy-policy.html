Privacy Policy for Itinera

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Itinera ("the App", "we", "us") is a travel itinerary app developed by YNOT Labs. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using Itinera you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and password. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google); we do not see or store your raw password.

Profile information. Name, surname, mobile number, date of birth, home address (street, city, postal code), and a profile photo, if you choose to add them.

Trip and travel content. Trip names, itinerary legs (cities, dates, transport type, booking references), checklist items, notes, and activities you create. City names you enter are converted to map coordinates (via the Google Maps API) purely to display routes and pins — Itinera does not access your device's GPS location.

Travel documents and photos. Images or PDFs you upload or capture (e.g. tickets, boarding passes, reservations) via your device's camera or photo library, and any profile photo you upload.

Fellow traveller information. If you add other people to a trip, we collect the name, email, and/or phone number you provide for them so they can be shown on the itinerary or invited to join.

Expense and settlement information. Expense descriptions, amounts, categories, and records of who paid whom to settle shared costs. Itinera only tracks these figures for splitting purposes — it does not process real payments, store card numbers, or move money.

Notifications. With your permission, we schedule local reminders and push notifications about upcoming trip activities.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • To store and sync your trips, documents, and profile across your devices
  • To display itineraries, maps, checklists, and expense splits
  • To send reminders and notifications you have enabled
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App

3. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only in the following cases:

  • With other travellers on a shared trip — trip content, your name, and relevant contact details are visible to other members you invite to that trip.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf, currently Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, and Cloud Storage for uploaded files) and the Google Maps Platform (for geocoding and map display), under their own data processing terms.
  • If required by law, to comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or respond to a valid legal request.

4. Data retention

We retain your account and trip data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual trips, documents, or travellers at any time within the App. To delete your account and associated data entirely, contact us at the email below.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile and trip data directly in the App.
  • You can delete trips, documents, or your account at any time.
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as access, correction, deletion, or portability of your data) under laws like the GDPR or CCPA. Contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Data security

We use industry-standard services (Google Firebase) with encryption in transit and access controls to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

Itinera is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

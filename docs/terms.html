Terms of Use & Community Guidelines

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Itinera ("the app", "we", "us") is a travel itinerary and ticket organizer published by YnotLabs. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to these Terms of Use and Community Guidelines.

Acceptable use

Itinera lets you plan trips privately and, if you choose, share a trip with people you invite so you can collaborate. When you share trips, you are responsible for the content you add (activities, documents, expenses, names, and notes) and for how you interact with other members.

Zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive behavior

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users on Itinera. You agree not to post, upload, or share content that is unlawful, hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit, defamatory, or that infringes anyone's rights, and not to harass, bully, impersonate, or abuse other members.

Reporting and blocking

  • Report — On any shared trip, open Travellers / Members, tap the menu next to a member, and choose Report Member to flag objectionable content or abusive behavior. Reports are sent to us for review.
  • Block — From the same menu, choose Block. A blocked member's activities, documents, and expenses are hidden from you on shared trips, and their identity is hidden until you unblock them.

How we act on reports

We review reports of objectionable content and abusive users and act within 24 hours by removing offending content and ejecting the responsible user from the service where warranted. Repeated or serious violations may result in permanent removal of the account.

Account deletion

You can delete your account and its associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app.

Contact

To report abuse, ask a question, or raise a concern, email ynotlabs.dev@gmail.com. We aim to respond promptly.

Delete Your Itinera Account

Last updated: 7 August 2026

You can permanently delete your Itinera account and its data directly from within the app. This page explains how, and exactly what happens when you do.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Itinera app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account.
  3. Tap your account, then tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

No separate web sign-in is needed — deletion happens entirely inside the app, immediately and without an approval delay.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, contact details, date of birth, address, profile photo)
  • Your login credentials and account itself
  • Every trip you own — including its itinerary, legs, checklist items, activities, expenses, settlements, and attached documents
  • Your personal packing/prep checklists

What isn't deleted

  • Trips you were only a member of (not the owner) aren't deleted — you're simply removed from them. The trip and its content remain for the owner and any other travellers.
  • Uploaded photos and documents (e.g. ticket scans) are stored with our media provider. The records pointing to them are deleted immediately, but the underlying files may take up to 30 days to be purged from backups.

Can't sign in to delete your account?

If you've lost access to your account and can't sign in to complete deletion yourself, email us and we'll process the request manually:
